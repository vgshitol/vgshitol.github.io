01 — About

Biodata & background

Researcher-engineer who works across the full stack — model, compiler, accelerator, and deployment — and across teams to ship on time.

How I work

I build models that have to survive real constraints: milliwatts, memory, latency, and noisy rooms. At Qualcomm that has meant training and distilling PyTorch models for always-on use, fixing the feature pipeline underneath them, and building the tooling so other teams can deploy without starting from scratch.

Before speech, I spent three years in XR R&D — hand and eye tracking, segmentation, super-resolution — and built the inference and quantization framework plus a custom MLIR code generator that turned ML layers into hardware kernels on the NPU. That hardware fluency still shapes how I design models today.

Across roles I have been the technical driver from R&D to production: coordinating cross-team goals, making the judgment calls on the right solution, and resolving customer issues through delivery.
